How to use Coullsey's Lucky Wheel

Viewers send a gift, your gift app calls the wheel's link, and the wheel spins on stream with their name. When it lands it can trigger a webhook (e.g. your reward server) and/or press a key in your game.

1. Put it on stream

In OBS or TikTok LIVE Studio add a Browser Source with the link from Settings → Overlay (http://localhost:5730/overlay), size 1080 × 1080. Keep this app open while you're live, as it runs the wheel. The wheel only shows while it's spinning unless you turn on Always show wheel.

2. Wheels

Pick a wheel from the tabs at the top, or press + New wheel. Each wheel has its own webhook link (Copy webhook) and can show as a roulette ◎ Wheel or a ▭ Bar.

Every box has a colour (click the swatch - it can also 🖼 Upload photo), a frequency (higher = more likely, the % shows its chance) and the prize text. When it lands it can:

• send its webhook (e.g. http://localhost:3000/webhook/reward1), and/or
• press its keybind: click ⌨ Record key then press the key (with Ctrl / Shift / Alt if you want). ▶ tests it after 3 seconds so you can click into your game first.

Both are optional and ✕ clears them. Press Save when you're done.

3. Connect your gift app

In TikFinity, DrewTok, etc. make the gift call the wheel's link, e.g. http://localhost:5730/wheel?id=0&userName={nickname} (use your app's username placeholder). In DrewTok set the webhook to send once, not per key press.

4. Play

Automatic ON = gift apps can spin the wheels. OFF = only Manual spin works. Manual spin lets you spin any wheel yourself. Live shows the queue and recent results. The preview is muted unless you press 🔇 Muted.

5. Customize

Change the font (built-in, or upload your own), the colours, and the prize / queue text. Changes show in the preview straight away; ▶ Demo spin tries them without rewards or keys. Press Save to put them on stream.

6. Settings

Spin time, how long the result shows, sounds, turning all webhooks or keybinds on/off, your overlay link, the app theme, and Open data folder (your wheels, photos, fonts and wheel.log). Put a Wheel Spin.mp3, Ding.mp3 or Coullsey Wheel.png in that folder to replace the sounds or logo.

Updates

The app checks for new versions when it opens. When one is ready a green Restart to update button appears at the top.

Tip: if a gift app doesn't spin the wheel, open wheel.log - every request that reaches the wheel is listed there.
